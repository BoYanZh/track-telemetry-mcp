# Post-session debrief and practice card

Use [the unified workflow](../docs/analysis-methodology.md), stages 1–6, and
`skill/SKILL.md`. Use `session_coach.md` for session-specific choices and
`reference_comparison.md` only when an external reference is supplied.
This is a compact presentation of complete analysis, not a reduced evidence gate.

## Compact delivery

State the next-session priority first. Give one or two located focuses, each with
one cue/action or validation, what stays fixed, success and when to stop.
Mention supported improvements and areas to leave alone only when they affect
that plan. Link to the complete report and evidence; a practice card should take
about one minute to read. Do not invent a driving correction to fill a card.

Complete both reports and JSON through `finalize_session_debrief` as described in
`docs/session-debrief.md`. Use the unified workflow's completion checklist.
